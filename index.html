Arrow Escape 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Arrow Escape 3D ("the game") is developed by Koukou. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Playing offline

The levels, the daily puzzle and duels with friends work without any account and send nothing to us. To remember your progress, the game saves your level, stars, coins, daily streak, duel record, duel nickname and sound settings on your device only. This data is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

When you challenge a friend, the game creates a message with a code containing the puzzle, your nickname and your result. It is shared only with the people you choose to send it to, through the app you choose (for example WhatsApp).

Online duels and the ladder

Only if you use Random opponent, the Ladder or Top players, the game connects to Google Firebase, which we use to store:

  • An anonymous player ID, created automatically. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number or Google account.
  • Your duel nickname, which other players see on the ladder and in duels. Don't use your real name if you don't want to.
  • Your duel results: blocks cleared, times, wins, losses and ladder points, and your progress during a live duel (which your opponent sees as it happens).
  • The highest level you have reached, shown with your nickname on the Top players board.

If you turn on Random duel requests (in the 1v1 menu, or by using Random opponent), your nickname is listed as available while the game is open, so other players can send you a duel request. You can turn it off at any time. While you send a request, your nickname is shown to the players you ask. This data is used only to match you with other players and to show the ladder. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not shared with anyone else. It is stored by Google Firebase (Google LLC): your nickname and results in the European Union, and the anonymous player ID on Google's sign-in servers. Everything is sent over encrypted connections. See Firebase's privacy information.

Deleting your online data

In the game, open 1v1 Duel → Ladder → Delete my online data. This permanently removes your player ID, nickname, ladder points, highest level and your duel records. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play.
  • Internet: used only for online duels, the ladder and app updates from Google Play.

No ads, no tracking

The game has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

If the game starts handling data in other ways, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
