Arrow Escape 3D – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

Arrow Escape 3D ("the game") is developed by Koukou. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

Information we collect

None. The game does not collect, store on any server, or share any personal information. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Data stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device only: your current level, stars earned, coins, daily puzzle streak, and sound and music settings. This data never leaves your device and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data.

Permissions

  • Vibration: used for haptic feedback during play.
  • Internet: included by the app framework. The game does not use it to send or receive any data, and it works fully offline.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example advertising), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
