Trapsmith – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Trapsmith is a game by koukou. This policy explains what the game does with your data. In short: it collects nothing.

What we collect

Nothing. Trapsmith does not ask for an account, does not collect personal information, and does not use analytics or advertising.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (your vault, gold, raid tickets, thief level and settings) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

Vault codes

You can copy a code of your vault and send it to a friend yourself. A code contains only the vault's layout, its traps and an optional short message you wrote. Nothing is sent unless you share it.

Updates

On Android, the game asks Google Play whether a newer version is available, so it can offer you the update. This uses Google Play's own update service; the game sends no data of its own.

Children

Trapsmith is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version starts collecting data (for example online play or ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the change will be described here.
